About AMV INFOCOM

Your Trusted Technology Infrastructure & Distribution Partner

Incorporated on 17th October 2013 under the Registrar of Companies (RoC Chennai), AMV INFOCOM PRIVATE LIMITED has developed an established track record in the distribution of IT hardware, computer peripheral equipment, database infrastructure, and enterprise maintenance services across South India.

Under the experienced management of our founders and directors, we bridge the gap between OEM manufacturers and corporate enterprises. Whether executing structured network cabling for multi-storey offices, deploying firewalls, providing point-of-sale software packages, or supplying servers for tender requirements, AMV INFOCOM prioritizes uncompromising quality, timely delivery, and dependable on-site technical support.
